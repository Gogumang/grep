**Job Description -- Sr. Director, TestOps**

Coupang operates a large distributed technology ecosystem where changes must be validated quickly, safely, and repeatedly across infrastructure and application boundaries. We are seeking a Director, TestOps to build and lead testing as a platform capability. This leader will own test automation, test environments and data, integration testing, resilience validation, quality signals, and AI-assisted testing. The mission is to move testing from a manual phase owned independently by teams into an automated, federated, and measurable platform that enables safe continuous delivery. We are looking for a visionary quality-platform leader who has previously built and scaled comparable TestOps, test infrastructure, or continuous-quality systems in a leading global technology company.

### Key Responsibilities

* Set a bold vision for testing as a company-wide platform capability and translate that vision into an executable roadmap adopted across infrastructure and domain organizations.  
* Define and execute the TestOps strategy across Tech Infrastructure and partner engineering organizations.  
* Build a federated testing platform that supports infrastructure, platform, API, UI, integration, resilience, and release validation.  
* Create repeatable test capabilities for Unified Staging and production-like environments.  
* Partner with Developer Platform, SRE, Observability, Security, and domain teams to embed quality gates into delivery workflows.  
* Own testing metrics including coverage, pass rate, escaped defects, flaky tests, execution time, environment readiness, and release confidence.  
* Drive adoption through product management, customer enablement, documentation, office hours, and clear service-level objectives.

### Test Platform \& Automation

* Build self-service test planning, execution, orchestration, reporting, and evidence-management capabilities.  
* Automate UI, API, service integration, infrastructure integration, and end-to-end test execution.  
* Provide reusable test harnesses, reference applications, simulators, mocks, and service-virtualization capabilities.  
* Integrate TestOps with CI/CD, Global CD, Application Registry, observability, and release governance.  
* Create quality gates that are risk-aware, fast, deterministic, and appropriate to service tiers.  
* Reduce flaky tests, duplicated frameworks, manual validation, and fragmented tooling.

### Unified Staging, Test Data \& Resilience Validation

* Use Unified Staging as the default environment for validating Tech Infrastructure components and domain applications.  
* Build compliant, desensitized, production-like test data generation and lifecycle management.  
* Enable integration testing across identity, mesh, networking, databases, queues, storage, observability, and application dependencies.  
* Partner with SRE to automate fault injection, DR scenarios, failover validation, load testing, and reliability certification.  
* Ensure validation evidence can feed release decisions, RBD/RBO certification, and executive reliability metrics.

### AI-Assisted Testing

* Build AI-assisted capabilities for feature test-plan generation, test-case generation, test-data generation, execution, failure analysis, and defect triage.  
* Establish evaluation, safety, and quality mechanisms for AI-generated tests and recommendations.  
* Use production telemetry and incident learnings to continuously improve test coverage and prioritize high-risk scenarios.  
* Automate quality insights and next-best-action recommendations for engineers and release owners.

### Talent Leadership \& Organization

* Lead managers, technical leads, and senior engineers across test platform, automation, and quality engineering.  
* Own organization design, hiring, leadership development, and succession planning.  
* Build a strong engineering culture focused on platform products rather than manual test execution.  
* Develop leaders who can independently own federation, test infrastructure, AI TestOps, and resilience validation.  
* Attract engineers with deep test infrastructure, distributed systems, developer tooling, and automation expertise.

### Technical Leadership \& Architecture

* Own architecture for test orchestration, environment integration, test data, result storage, quality signals, and release gating.  
* Define company standards for test APIs, frameworks, evidence, coverage, flakiness, and service-tier validation.  
* Influence platform architecture to improve testability, observability, fault isolation, and safe deployment.  
* Align test strategy with the broader observability and AIOps architecture so pre-release and post-release quality signals reinforce one another.

### Execution \& Impact

* Deliver measurable reductions in manual testing, execution time, escaped defects, flaky tests, and release-related incidents.  
* Increase automated integration and resilience validation across Tier 0, Tier 1, and Tech Infrastructure services.  
* Improve release confidence and developer productivity through reliable self-service testing.  
* Establish strong execution mechanisms, customer feedback loops, and objective quarterly targets.

### Essential Qualifications

* Leadership experience in a best-in-class test infrastructure, developer productivity, continuous delivery, or reliability engineering organization at hyperscaler, global consumer platform, or similarly scaled technology company.  
* Experience with Kubernetes, cloud-native testing, service mesh, fault injection, or chaos engineering.  
* Experience building AI-assisted test generation or failure-analysis systems.  
* Experience with load testing, DR validation, release certification, or production-readiness programs.  
* 12+ years of experience in software engineering, test infrastructure, developer productivity, quality engineering, or platform engineering.  
* 5+ years leading engineering teams and managers in a large-scale technology environment.  
* Recognized as a strong technology and organizational leader with the ability to define a differentiated quality engineering vision, attract exceptional talent, and influence senior stakeholders.  
* Demonstrated experience personally leading the architecture, build-out, launch, and scaled adoption of a TestOps, test infrastructure, continuous-quality, or release-validation platform.  
* Experience building test automation or testing platforms for distributed systems and continuous delivery.  
* Deep understanding of CI/CD, test environments, test data, integration testing, reliability testing, and quality metrics.  
* Experience driving cross-organizational adoption of common platforms and standards.  
* Strong technical judgment, customer focus, and ability to translate strategy into measurable delivery.
