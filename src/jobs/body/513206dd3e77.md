**Coupang Finance Development Program**

Coupang Finance Development Program is designed to recruit smart and talented MBA graduates from top universities and develop them to be finance experts at Coupang. Trainees will experience 6months of rotation work in core teams within Coupang Finance and will ultimately be assigned to the most suitable team after 12months.

(1) Corporate Development

: Search, evaluate, and secure external opportunities such as talent, technology, business, and products i) to identify new customer pain points and develop solutions for them, and ii) to accelerate the resolution of existing problems the team Coupang is working on

(2) Logistics Finance

: In charge of planning, monitoring and advising business from finance perspectives, including annual/long-term planning to weekly/monthly/quarterly reviews, bringing total transparency to key business leaders

(3) Rocket Growth Finance

: FP\&A function that supports Rocket Growth business which helps sellers grow through Coupang's best delivery experience and customer service

(4) Corporate FP\&A

: Directs the company-wide forecast and budget process, analyses variance to these plans and forecasts and provides insights to the leadership team. Supports the Investor Relations team to inform and communicate with investors and investment analysts

(5) Play Finance

: Responsible for budgeting, forecasting, and strategic resource allocation for Coupang Play. Partner with business leaders to evaluate financial performance against subscriber and engagement metrics, identify growth opportunities, and provide data-driven recommendations that enhance profitability, capital efficiency, and long-term business value.

(6) CFS Finance

: Lead financial planning and analysis for Coupang Fulfillment Services by evaluating network capacity, labor efficiency, and operational investments. Provide visibility into key cost drivers and business performance to enable strategic decision-making across fulfillment operations.
