**Role Overview**

The Traffic Management team is an integral part of the Technical Infrastructure and Platform organization and is responsible for building traffic management systems that are leveraged across the entire company. Since the platforms we build are key to the success of a broad set of backend engineering teams, we focus on systems that provide high availability (5 '9s) and extremely low latency. We are also pioneering efforts related to enhancing developer productivity using AI. We are looking for a Staff software engineer who is highly motivated and experienced in building state-of-the-art solutions that deliver business value.
