**Team introduction**

Quality Assurance team plays a vital role in elevating customer satisfaction by ensuring garment excellence across all fashion lines. The Senior Quality Assurance Specialist must dive deep into technical specs and lead fitting sessions to resolve complex garment construction issues, while establishing robust quality control standards and maintaining seamless collaboration with internal partners and manufacturing suppliers.

**Responsibilities**

* Manage end-to-end fitting and spec validation across 1st, 2nd, and PP sample stages, including pre-production approvals and inspection of fabric, stitching, and finishing for new product launches.  
* Lead dress-form and live fitting sessions with Designers and Brand Managers, solving technical construction issues and distributing detailed comments with photos, diagrams, and spec adjustment guides.  
* Establish technical standards by managing tolerances and grading specs, and defining standardized construction and trim guidelines.  
  Drive cross-functional vendor communication by sharing revised specs and tech packs with factories, resolving technical queries, and tracking ongoing revisions.

**Qualifications**

* Experience: Minimum 8 years of experience in Apparel Technical Design or Apparel QA.  
* Deep understanding of garment patterns, sewing construction, grading specs, and tech pack creation.  
* Ability to conduct rigorous measurements and quality inspections based on quality standard guidelines (such as CPLB standards).  
* Strong technical communication skills with domestic and overseas factories/vendors.

**Preferred skills**

* Data-driven problem solver capable of analyzing customer feedback (e.g., return reasons) to deliver actionable fit and spec improvements.  
* English communication skills for global factory alignment.
