**회사 소개**

쿠팡은 고객 감동 실현을 위해 존재합니다. 고객들이 "쿠팡 없이 그동안 어떻게 살았을까?" 라고 말할 때, 비로소 우리의 미션을 실현하고 있음을 알 수 있습니다. 고객들의 쇼핑과 식사, 생활 전반을 편하게 만들겠다는 유일한 집념으로 쿠팡은 수억 달러 규모의 이커머스 산업 전반의 혁신을 이끌고 있습니다. 쿠팡은 가장 빠르게 성장하는 이커머스 기업 중 하나로, 국내 커머스 업계에서의 독보적인 입지와, 고객 신뢰를 구축했습니다.

쿠팡은 스타트업 문화를 기반으로 한 글로벌 대형 상장사라고 자부합니다. 이것이 창립 당시의 기민함을 지하며, 신규 서비스를 끊임없이 출시하며 비즈니스를 확장해 나가는 우리의 성장 동력입니다. 쿠팡의 모든 임직원에게는 기업가 정신을 갖추고 새로운 혁신과 이니셔티브를 추진할 수 있는 기회가 주어집니다. 주저 없이 일에 뛰어들어 성과를 이루고자 하는 과감성이, 바로 쿠팡이 일하는 방식의 본질입니다. 쿠팡에서는 여러분 자신, 동료, 팀 그리고 회사 전체가 매일 성장하는 모습을 목격할 것입니다.

쿠팡의 모든 직원은 커머스의 미래를 만들겠다는 쿠팡의 미션에 진심입니다. 우리는 고객의 문제를 해결해 나가고, 전통적인 관념과 통념에 맞서며 실현 가능한 한계를 뛰어넘고 있습니다. 고가용성 (always-on) 과 최첨단의 앞선 기술 (high-tech), 초연결사회 (hyper-connected world) 에서의 놀라운 업무 경험을 원하신다면, 지금 바로 쿠팡에 합류하세요.

**Coupang Fulfillment Transportation Systems**

The Fulfillment Transportation Systems Tech organization within Global Operations Technology is responsible for systems that enable Coupang to reliably deliver customer orders within hours of order placement. Transportation Tech is on a mission to transform Last Miles by building an efficient Trans ecosystem that operates at groundbreaking scale and speed. We can drive technology direction, successfully advocate for collaboration with other technology leaders and stakeholders, influence and build trust with teams, and execute complex system delivery with consideration for scalability, future scalability, and reliability. We are looking for an experienced and passionate engineering leader. As the organization in charge of Coupang's Last Miles, we develop solutions and provide products for the systems used by those who directly deliver within Coupang and the managers who manage them. In addition, we provide Admin and Mobile WebView to allocate supplies to Coupang Friends and Coupang Flex workers and manage Coupang Flex's work, meta information, and settlement information.

The candidate will be part of the Coupang Delivery Technology team and will be responsible for developing and operating solutions for systems and terminals used by delivery people and managers within Coupang.

**Key Responsibilities:**

-   Sr, Staff front-end engineers are technology leaders and participate in major technology improvement projects.  
-   After understanding the overall and details of the Coupang GOT web platform and identifying areas for improvement, we present an architecture-level solution.  
-   Additionally, we will create a roadmap with other engineers and implement technological improvements step by step, and smooth communication to align with other developers or organizations will be essential in this process.  
-   By collaborating closely with the Product Owner / Designer / Stakeholder, we simplify complex business requirements and complete them into a high-level front-end design to support multiple repeated tests with minimal development costs.  
-   There are places that require a variety of front-end developer capabilities, such as pages that require handling and visualization of large amounts of data.  
-   We serve as mentors for members within the organization and help them improve their individual capabilities.

**Skills and Qualifications:**

-   10+ years of experience working in basic algorithms, system design, and large-scale distributed systems.  
-   12+ years of professional coding experience using modern programming languages such as JavaScript, Typescript, etc.  
-   Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field.  
-   Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail Ability to work in an agile development environment and meet tight deadlines.

**Preferred:**

-   Experience working in building complex software systems that have been successfully delivered to customers.  
-   Experience communicating with users, other technical teams, and senior management to collect requirements, and describe software product features, technical designs, and product strategy.  
-   Experience influencing software engineering best practices within your team Hands-on expertise in many disparate technologies, ranging from front-end user interfaces and all points in between Experience working backward from the customer and delivering innovative, simple solutions
