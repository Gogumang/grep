**Role Overview**

The Principal Technical Compliance Manager -- IT SOX is a **director‑level individual contributor** responsible for defining and executing the enterprise IT SOX strategy across **Coupang Engineered Systems** . Reporting to the Director of Technical Compliance, this role owns **end‑to‑end IT SOX program strategy, planning, execution, and operational excellence**, ensuring a consistent, scalable, and high‑quality control environment across all SOX‑in‑scope systems.

This role operates as a **technical program leader** , driving strategy and influencing senior engineering and platform leaders. A key focus is leveraging **automation and AI‑enabled tooling** to scale risk management, improve compliance observability, and reduce manual effort while increasing audit confidence. Success requires deep technical fluency in modern distributed systems, strong SOX ITGC expertise, and the ability to scale compliance programs in high‑growth, technology‑first environments.

**Responsibilities**

* ***Program Ownership \& Strategy***

  * Own the I**T SOX compliance strategy** across all engineered systems, including multi‑year roadmap development, prioritization, and execution of SOX‑impacting initiatives

  * Serve as the **single‑threaded owner** for IT SOX program outcomes, quality, and continuous improvement

  * Define a strategic vision for **scaling IT risk management and compliance observability,** including the effective use of **AI, analytics, and automation** to enhance control coverage and reduce operational friction

* ***Metrics, Operational Excellence \& Scaling***

  * Establish clear **program goals and success metrics**, including:

    * Output goals aligned to audit quality, risk reduction, and control reliability

    * Input metrics that drive predictable and scalable execution

    * Feedback loops that validate outcomes and continuously iterate program design

  * Design and implement **operational working models**, SOPs, and SLAs to maximize efficiency and throughput across ITGC design and testing

  * Identify and drive opportunities to **leverage AI‑enabled tools** (e.g., intelligent evidence validation, anomaly detection, control monitoring) to improve risk visibility and testing consistency at scale

* ***Cross‑Functional Leadership \& Influence***

  * Partner with senior engineering, infrastructure, security, and corporate IT leaders to **plan, prioritize, and deliver SOX‑impacting initiatives**

  * Influence technical design and operational decisions to proactively mitigate financial reporting and technology risks

  * Act as a **trusted advisor** to technology leadership on IT risk, controls, and the adoption of automation or AI solutions to improve compliance outcomes

* ***Technical, Risk \& AI‑Enabled Compliance Expertise***

  * Develop and maintain a deep understanding of Coupang's **platform infrastructure, service‑oriented and distributed architectures**, and how engineered systems integrate with Corporate IT for financial reporting

  * Drive improvements in **compliance observability**, including real‑time or near‑real‑time visibility into control health, execution, and risk signals

  * Identify, assess, and respond to **emerging IT risks, regulatory changes, and industry trends**, including those introduced by AI‑driven systems and automation

  * Provide technical leadership in evaluating control design for **cloud‑native, containerized, and AI‑augmented environments**

  * Demonstrated experience operating at a **director or principal IC level**, influencing senior stakeholders without people management responsibility
