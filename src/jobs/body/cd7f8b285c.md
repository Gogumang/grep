**Team Overview**

The **Platform Services team** within E-commerce Engineering builds, operates, and scales the core backend platform powering essential logic of Coupang’s mobile app and web—includes Auth, Member, Product Reviews, Search, Category, Product Detail, Cart, Checkout, and Orders. This role leads the technical direction and architecture of the next-generation global platform that delivers reliable and secured customer experience across Korea, Taiwan, Japan, and other global markets.

**Role Overview**

As a **Senior Staff Backend Engineer** (IC), you will shape the long-term technical strategy and architecture of the core platform domains in E-commerce Engineering. You will build infrastructure and global strategy for the domains that are responsible for customer PII, defining and evangelizing organizational standards and best practices while designing and implementing highly reliable and scalable systems, and mentoring Senior and Staff engineers to raise the technical bar.

**Key Responsibilities**

-   Drive Technology Vision & Roadmap: Define and champion a 12–18 month platform strategy, author and approve RFCs, and align cross-functional teams toward shared objectives.  
-   Architectural Authority: Provide thought leadership in designing privacy compliant architectures for multi-region, multi-tenant environments. Establish and enforce API governance standards and controlling access, monitoring with detection and response.  
-   Lead Complex System Design: Oversee architecture that ensures backward compatibility, orchestrates phased migrations, and maintains data integrity—including eventual consistency—while optimizing caching strategies.  
-   Operational Excellence & Reliability: Set and uphold SLOs and error budgets. Lead capacity planning, performance optimization, and incident management, including root-cause analysis and postmortem reviews.  
-   Security, Compliance & Cost Optimization: Ensure privacy and regulatory compliance while driving cost-efficient technical decisions and operational standards.  
-   Hands-on Contribution: Perform high-impact code reviews and performance optimizations.  
-   Cross-Functional Influence: Partner with PMs, TPMs, and teams across Search, Data, Checkout, and more to deliver reusable platform components and standards.  
-   Mentorship & Bar Raising: Coach Senior and Staff level engineers, lead design reviews, and elevate engineering culture and quality standards.
