**Role Overview**  
The Gateway Services team is responsible for building Coupang's traffic infrastructure layer leveraging Service Mesh architecture to enable reliable, scalable, and efficient inter-service communication across the company. This includes critical capabilities such as service discovery, load balancing, traffic resiliency, traffic management, failure recovery, and observability. As a Senior Staff Software Engineer, you will play a critical role in designing and evolving the Service Mesh platform that powers Coupang's services at scale. You will lead the development of fine-grained, enterprise-wide traffic control systems using state-of-the-art technologies such as Istio, Envoy, Kubernetes, ArgoCD, and SPIFFE. In this role, you will not only drive architectural decisions and deliver highly scalable infrastructure but also act as a thought leader and evangelist, promoting best practices in traffic management across the organization. You will collaborate with cross-functional platform teams to solve complex infrastructure challenges and establish a modern, extensible platform that supports Coupang's rapid growth.

**What You Will Do**  
• Design and implement Service Mesh architecture and fine-grained traffic control systems in large-scale microservices environments  
• Develop and enhance core infrastructure components related to traffic management, including service discovery, load balancing, and resiliency mechanisms  
• Build and operate Service Mesh components using technologies such as Istio and Envoy within Kubernetes environments  
• Architect and develop workload and service management infrastructure for large-scale distributed systems

• Act as a technical leader and evangelist, promoting Service Mesh and traffic management best practices across the organization
