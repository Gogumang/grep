**About the Role

** Lead ML innovations on Retrieval and Relevance for Coupang Eats Search, one of Asia's fastest-growing commerce businesses. You will shape how customers discover brands, stores, dishes, and products across food delivery and quick commerce, owning the machine learning models and strategies behind retrieval and relevance. As the ML Lead for Coupang Eats Search, you will be accountable for increasing successful retrieval of brands, stores, dishes, and products, and for driving measurable improvements in relevance and conversion. You will own the end-to-end stack, including query and menu understanding, candidate retrieval, relevance, and quality evaluation. You will establish a multi-year roadmap and lead projects across machine learning and engineering. Food-delivery search is dynamic, hyperlocal, and time-sensitive. Results must reflect customer intent while adapting to location, restaurant and menu availability, delivery time, price, promotions, quality, and real-time customer context. The ideal candidate combines deep search and NLP expertise with strong product judgment and organizational leadership, and can balance quality, latency, reliability, and experimentation velocity.

**What You Will Do (Key Responsibilities)**  
* **Customer and Business Outcomes**: Own the machine learning strategy, metrics, and execution for improving brand, store, dish, and product retrieval and increasing conversion across Coupang Eats Search surfaces. Establish clear outcome metrics and targets and use rigorous experimentation and diagnostics to deliver sustained improvements while protecting key guardrail metrics and customer experience.

* **Query and Product Understanding:** improve query intent and entity understanding, menu and restaurant representation, typo tolerance, synonym expansion, and query rewriting.

* **Retrieval:** Advance lexical, semantic, and hybrid retrieval. design scalable, low-latency retrieval strategies, including indexing, feature pipelines, model life cycle.

* **Relevance Measurement and Experimentation:** Establish a rigorous quality framework using offline evaluation, human judgments, online experiments, guardrail metrics, and diagnostics for tail queries, cold-start cases, and customer segments.
