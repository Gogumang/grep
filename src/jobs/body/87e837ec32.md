**Role Overview**

We are seeking a highly motivated Program Manager(PM) to support the execution of strategic initiatives across Coupang Eats. Eats Engineering PMO sets the direction for our core products and services and serves as the hub that aligns teams around shared goals. To safeguard business continuity, vendor management, SLA and performance, incident escalation, and stakeholder communications to ensure consistent service quality. as well as lead project/program end to end delivery and enable timely, high‑quality delivery through planning and execution to bring critical business impact to Eats.

**What You Will Do**

-   Program/Project Delivery Support: lead project/program end to end delivery and enable timely, high‑quality delivery through planning and execution.

-   Vendor Portfolio Operations: Maintain vendor inventory and tiering; keep standard artifacts current.
