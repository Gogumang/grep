**\[회사 소개\]**

쿠팡은 고객이 쿠팡앱을 열어보는 순간부터 상품을 문 앞에서 받아보는 순간까지, 고객 한 분 한 분에게 감동을 전달하기 위해 쇼핑 경험을 혁신하고 있습니다.

엔드투엔드(end-to-end) 이커머스 및 물류 네트워크와 고객 중심의 집요한 문화에 기반하여, 쿠팡은 속도, 상품 다양성, 가격 경쟁력 모두를 동시에 달성해 왔습니다. 신선식품을 포함한 수백만 개의 상품을 전국 어디든 단 몇 시간 내 배송하는 서비스를 연중무휴로 제공하며 커머스 시장의 새로운 기준을 만들어가고 있습니다.

급성장하는 한국 커머스 시장에서 쿠팡은 기술과 운영 혁신을 통해 고객 경험의 미래를 만들어가고 있습니다.

**\[Position Overview\]**

We are looking for a skilled and experienced Database Engineer with AI experience to join the Customer Success team at Coupang. The Customer Success team obsesses over the success of development and application teams by delivering high-quality storage infrastructure. In this role, you will be responsible for developing tools and automation (scripts, AI) for reliable operations of Aurora MySQL, Aurora PostgreSQL, DocumentDB, DynamoDB, Cassandra, and TiDB databases in a high-traffic, mission-critical e-commerce environment. You will work closely with development teams and application teams to ensure database reliability, performance, and availability while driving operational excellence through automation and best practices.

-   Opportunity to manage large-scale databases on one of the largest e-commerce platforms.  
-   Exposure to cutting-edge AWS database technologies and cloud-native architectures.  
-   A collaborative and diverse team environment with global colleagues.  
-   Continuous learning and professional development opportunities.

**\[Key Responsibilities\]**

-   **AI Automation**

-   Generate AI Skills and Context prompts to automate storage engine and code and configuration generation across storage technologies.

-   **Database Operations & Management**

-   Manage and operate large-scale Amazon Aurora MySQL and Aurora PostgreSQL clusters in production environments.  
-   Perform database maintenance tasks including version upgrades (minor/major), patching, and parameter tuning.  
-   Execute Blue/Green Deployment (BGD) based upgrades and migrations with minimal downtime.

-   **Performance Optimization**

-   Monitor and analyze database performance using AWS CloudWatch, Performance Insights, and internal monitoring tools.  
-   Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks through query optimization, index tuning, and configuration adjustments.

-   **Reliability & Availability**

-   Ensure high availability and reliability of database services through proactive monitoring and incident response.  
-   Participate in on-call rotations and respond to database-related incidents in a timely manner.  
-   Design and implement failover strategies and validate recovery procedures.

-   **Automation & Tooling**

-   Develop and maintain automation scripts and tools (Python) for database operations, reducing manual intervention.  
-   Create and maintain Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for all database operations.  
-   Contribute to internal tooling and operational frameworks.

-   **Collaboration**
