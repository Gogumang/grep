**Role Overview**

The **Global Operations Tech (GOT) organization** is responsible for the systems that enable Coupang to reliably deliver to customers within hours of orders being placed. GOT is on a mission to innovate logistics by building an efficient delivery network at breakthrough scale and speeds. As a Staff Backend Engineer in GOT, you will leverage advanced algorithms to improve Coupang’s customer experience by ensuring ultra-fast deliveries are on time even during the busiest times of the year.

**What You Will Do**

-   Design and develop system architecture for Global Operations Technology to ensure stable operations.  
-   Develop front/back office web services based on the Java platform.  
-   Identify dependencies across multiple domains and develop system integrations.  
-   Evaluate and adopt new technologies to enable efficient development and operations.  
-   Implement optimization algorithms to find the most cost-effective and fastest delivery methods based on rule-based logic.

**Qualifications**

-   Experience developing web applications using Java and Spring Framework.  
-   7+ years of programming experience.  
-   5+ years of hands-on experience in web application development and operations, or equivalent capabilities.  
-   Interest in experiencing Coupang’s global-level logistics systems and diverse domains.  
-   Passion for developing high-availability systems that handle large-scale traffic in a microservices architecture (MSA) environment.

**Preferred Qualifications**

-   Experience or understanding of microservices architecture-based system development.  
-   Experience or understanding of service development and operations in cloud environments.  
-   Experience with asynchronous event-driven architecture for large-scale processing.  
-   Experience improving code quality through development best practices.  
-   Experience with ORM tools such as JPA or Hibernate and domain modeling.  
-   Experience developing e-commerce services.  
-   Experience with build/test/deployment automation.  
-   Experience handling large-scale data using NoSQL technologies (e.g., Memcached, Redis).
